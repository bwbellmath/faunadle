How to play

Every day there is a mystery species, one of in this game. Guess any species by its common or scientific name. Every guess is placed on the tree of life against the mystery species:

  • Diverged: how long ago your guess and the mystery species shared their last common ancestor (45M = 45 million years, 320K = 320,000 years). Smaller is closer.
  • Links: how many branch points you pass through on the tree to get from your guess to the mystery species. Sister species are 1 link apart.
  • Shared group: the smallest named group (genus, family, order…) that contains both.

Guesses are sorted by divergence time, then links. Tap a guess to see the groups it shares with the mystery species. Hints reveal the order, family, genus, and the first letter of the common name, one at a time.
